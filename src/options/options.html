Options & data controls

Benchmark source

The only benchmark source is PassMark: CPU Mark for processors (cpubenchmark.net) and G3D Mark for graphics cards (videocardbenchmark.net). Scores are read verbatim from PassMark and are never estimated. No other benchmark database is ever used.

Cache

Retrieved devices are cached locally. Results older than this are refreshed from PassMark automatically; you can also refresh any device manually at any time.

Privacy
